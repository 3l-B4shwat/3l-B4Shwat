Who is 3l B4Shwat?

We are an elite independent CTF team and offensive security research collective born from late nights, curiosity, and an obsession with understanding how digital systems break. Named after the historic Ottoman fez symbol worn with modern technological rebellion, 3l B4Shwat bridges classical computer science principles with cutting-edge exploit engineering.

Whether it's unearthing 0-days in complex web stacks, reversing heavily obfuscated binaries, or cracking cryptographic primitives under strict time pressure, our focus is uncompromising: precision, mastery, and transparent sharing of knowledge.
